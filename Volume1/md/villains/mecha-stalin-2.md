# Mecha-Stalin 2

## Abilities

### [A] [Other] "No Man - No Problem"
Lower two of your Powers by one die size each. Increase one of your other powers to d12. Then take a basic action using that power.

### [I] [Boost] "Show My True Strength"
On your turn, whenever you Attack a target you haven’t dealt damage to yet this scene, also Boost yourself using your Max die.

### [R] [Attack] "Soviet Block"
When a nearby ally would be Attacked, you may become the target of that Attack instead. You may use this reaction any number of times in a round by taking 1 irreducible damage for each time past the first.

### [A] [Attack] "Steel Gloves"
Attack using Strength. If you are Green status, use your Max die. If you are Yellow status, use the Max+Min dice. If you are Red status, use Max+Min against one target and Mid against another.

### [A] [Defend] "The New Soviet Power"
Boost using Presence and use your Max die. Attack with your Mid die. Defend with your Min die.

## Upgrades

### [I] [Other] "New Soviet Hover-Tank"
Mecha-Stalin has the use of cutting-edge military technology, including a d10 hover-tank lieutenant with the Sturdy, Bombard, and Minion Deployment abilities from page 236. (When using this upgrade, Mecha-Stalin has 15 additional Health.)

## Mastery

### [I] [Overcome] "Master of Superiority"
As long as you are manifesting effects related to a power you have at max die, automatically succeed at an Overcome involving usage of those powers.
