# 9. Step 5 - Red Abilities
Choose two Red abilities. The Red abilities are grouped by power and quality category, but not just for convenience. You must have a die assigned to a power or quality in a category to use with an ability from that category. If an ability doesn’t use a power or quality at all (like most Inherent abilities), you must have a power or quality rated d6 or higher in that category in order to take it. 

Some Red abilities also specify that they can only be used with a specific power or quality instead of any from within that category.
