# Power Sources — Complete Reference (Step 2)
Source: printed pg.56–70. Entries 2–5 confirmed by directly reading page images; entries 6–20 read from text extraction after confirming the page layout is clean sequential blocks (Name+assign → Yellow table → Green table, one entry per block, no cross-page jumbling). Entry 1's ability names weren't captured in this pass — only its power list is confirmed.

## How it works
At the end of the Background step, you have a set of dice (size only, not rolled values). Use a single die's value or the sum of any two to pick a Power Source 1–20. Each grants: powers to assign your dice to, two Yellow abilities (pick 2, each tied to a different power) or occasionally a different bonus instead, usually one Green ability (pick 1) or a quality/power bonus instead, and new dice for Step 3 (Archetype).

## Full Entries

### 1 — Accident
*"An external source caused you to manifest powers or perhaps the cure for an accident caused it."*
Ability names not captured in this pass — powers list confirmed above.

### 2 — Training
*"The source of your powers is the result of your hard work, dedication, and long hours."*
**Yellow (pick 2, different powers):** Always Be Prepared (A) — Boost yourself using [power], persistent+exclusive, then Attack with Min die using that bonus. Reactive Field (R) — when attacked by a nearby enemy, attacker also takes equal damage. Flowing Fight (A) — Attack using [power] with Mid die, one extra target per bonus you have, different bonus applied each.
**Special:** when you move to the next step, select an extra quality from that archetype's list.

### 3 — Genetic
*"Mutations in your DNA have caused you to develop unusual abilities."*
**Yellow (pick 2):** Danger Sense (R) — Defend vs. environment/surprise damage by rolling [power]. Adaptive (A) — Boost using [power], then remove a penalty or Recover with Min die. Area Assault (A) — Attack multiple targets using [power], Min die each.
**Green (pick 1):** Growth (A) — Boost yourself using [quality], persistent+exclusive. Rally (A) — Attack using [quality]; nearby Yellow/Red-zone heroes Recover equal to your Min die.

### 4 — Experimentation
*"Your powers were created in a lab and had some unexpected side effects."*
**Yellow (pick 2):** Personal Upgrade (A) — Boost yourself using [power], Max die, persistent+exclusive. Misdirection (R) — Defend a nearby Yellow/Red-zone ally by rolling [power], redirect remainder to a nearby minion. Throw Minion (A) — Attack a minion using [power]; its save result Attacks another target of your choice.
**Green (pick 1):** Overpower (I) — whenever Boosted, +1 to that bonus; if it hits +5, take that much damage and remove it. Unflagging (I) — remove a penalty on yourself at the start of your turn.

### 5 — Mystical
*"Your magical training or alteration by magic gives you your powers."*
**Yellow (pick 2):** Modification Wave (A) — Boost or Hinder using [power], apply to multiple nearby targets. Mystic Redirection (R) — redirect a nearby Yellow/Red-zone ally's damage to yourself, Defend by rolling [power]. Sever Link (A) — Overcome an environmental challenge using [power], Max die; remove a scene penalty or Boost by Mid die.
**Special:** gain an Information quality instead of a Green ability.

### 6 — Nature
*"The power of nature flows through you."*
**Yellow (pick 2):** Call to the Wild (A) — gain a minion; acts before you, gone at scene end, one at a time max. Predator's Eye (A) — Attack using [power], Max+Min dice, Boost with Mid die; target gains an equal bonus. Wild Strength (R) — when you defeat a minion, roll its die and Boost yourself with the result.
**Green (pick 1):** Grasping Vines (A) — Hinder using [power], Max die, splittable across targets. Natural Weapon (A) — Attack using [power], Max die.

### 7 — Relic
*"An object (or collection of objects) of mystical significance either grants you powers or altered you to give you powers."*
**Yellow (pick 2):** Harvest Life Force (A) — Attack using [power] Min die, take Mid die damage yourself, ally Recovers Max die. Magical Shield (R) — Defend a Yellow/Red-zone ally by rolling [power]. Momentary Power (A) — Boost yourself Max die, Hinder a nearby opponent Min die. Relic Drain (A) — Hinder using [power], also Recover Min die.
**Green (pick 1):** Draw Power (A) — Boost yourself using [power], persistent+exclusive. Punishment (I) — Attacking an enemy who Hindered you treats that penalty as a bonus for the Attack.

### 8 — Powered Suit
*"An engineered suit provides you with your powers, and may even be important to keeping you alive."* **Power Suit is required** (assign one die to it first).
**Yellow (pick 2):** Energy Converter (R) — damage from [element] becomes a Boost instead. Explosive Attack (A) — Attack up to 3 targets, Max/Mid/Min split; doubles = minor twist or irreducible damage. Onboard Upgrade (A) — Boost yourself with Power Suit, Min+Mid dice, persistent+exclusive.
**Green (pick 1):** Damage Reduction (I) — reduce [physical/energy] damage by 1/2/3 per zone. Diagnostic Subroutine (I) — when your status changes, may remove a penalty on yourself.

### 9 — Radiation
*"Exposure to radiation has charged your system and given you new abilities."*
**Yellow (pick 2):** Radioactive Recharge (A) — Boost yourself, then remove a penalty or Recover Min die. Unstable Reaction (R) — after rolling, take 1 irreducible damage to reroll entire pool. Wither (A) — Attack using [power], Hinder same target Max die.
**Green (pick 1):** Charged Up (I) — rolling a 1 lets you reroll that die (must accept). Dangerous Lash (A) — Attack multiple targets Min die each; doubles = also Attack an ally Mid die. Radioactive Aura (R) — Attack a new nearby arrival by rolling [power].

### 10 — Tech Upgrades
*"You have technological upgrades and implants that give you your powers."*
**Yellow (pick 2):** Energy Burst (A) — Attack multiple targets, Min die each. Recharge (A) — Boost yourself, then remove a penalty or Recover Min die. Techno-Absorb (I) — damage from [element] may Recover instead. Tactical Analysis (R) — damage taken from an Attack becomes a Boost.
**Green (pick 1):** Indiscriminate Fabrication (A) — Boost with Min/Mid/Max as 3 separate bonuses, one must go to an enemy. Organi-Hack (A) — Attack using [power], Hinder same target Min die.

### 11 — Supernatural
*"In some way, you have pierced the veil of life and reality and brought back power."*
**Yellow (pick 2):** Area Healing (A) — Boost an ally; you + nearby Yellow/Red heroes Recover Min die. Mass Modification (A) — Boost or Hinder, apply to multiple close targets. Personal Upgrade (A) — Boost yourself Max die, persistent+exclusive. Reach through Veil (R) — Defend a nearby ally by rolling your status die, move them elsewhere in scene.
**Special:** gain one power not on the source's list instead of a Green ability.

### 12 — Artificial Being
*"You were created, not born, and your abilities simply stem from your makeup."*
**Yellow (pick 2):** Created Immunity (I) — damage from [element] may Recover instead. Multiple Assault (A) — Attack multiple targets Min die each. Recalculating... (R) — take 1 irreducible damage to reroll entire pool after rolling.
**Green (pick 1):** Created Form (I) — reduce physical damage 1/2/3 per zone. Intentionality (I) — reroll any die that shows a 1 (must accept).

### 13 — Cursed
*"A supernatural curse has been inflicted upon you or your family line, granting both boons and banes."*
**Yellow (pick 2):** Attunement (I) — damage from [element] may Recover instead. Costly Strength (A) — Boost all nearby allies Max+Mid dice, Hinder yourself Min die. Cursed Resolve (A) — Boost yourself, then remove a penalty or Recover Min die.
**Green (pick 1):** Double Edged Luck (I) — reroll any die showing a 1 (must accept). Extremes (I) — max die value treated as 1 higher; a rolled 1 treated as 0.

### 14 — Alien
*"You are not from Earth, though your powers might not be all that unusual where you come from. Or you've been granted abilities by an extraterrestrial source."*
**Yellow (pick 2):** Alien Boost (A) — Boost all nearby allies Max+Mid, Hinder yourself Min die. Empower and Repair (A) — Boost/Hinder/Defend/Attack using [power]; you + nearby Yellow/Red heroes Recover Min die. Halt (R) — Defend against a close Attack by rolling [power].
**Special:** upgrade one power/quality one size (or add a new power from the list if none qualify), instead of a Green ability.

### 15 — Genius
*"The source of your powers is your brilliant mind. You have put your staggering intellect to the task of fighting crime."*
**Yellow (pick 2):** A Plan For Everything (R) — when attacked, Defend then Boost yourself with the same roll. Expanded Mind (A) — Boost yourself Max die, persistent+exclusive, then Attack Min die. Overwhelming Vision (A) — Attack; if target survives, Attack again Max die, else Recover Min die.
**Special:** select an extra quality from Information or Mental category, instead of a Green ability.

### 16 — Cosmos
*"Exposure to forces from beyond the stars have changed you."*
**Yellow (pick 2):** Cosmic Ray Absorption (I) — damage from an [element] you have a related power for reduces to 0, Recover that amount. Encourage (A) — Attack, Boost all nearby heroes' Attack/Overcome actions Min die until next turn. Mass Effect (A) — Boost or Hinder, apply to multiple close targets.
**Special:** downgrade one power/quality one size, upgrade a different one one size, instead of a Green ability.

### 17 — Extradimensional
*"Exposure to side dimensions like the Realm of Discord has left its mark on you."*
**Yellow (pick 2):** Absorb Essence (R) — defeating a minion, roll its die, Boost yourself. Aura of Pain (A) — Attack multiple targets, take irreducible damage equal to targets hit. Bizarre Strike (A) — Attack Max die, Hinder same target Mid die, Hinder yourself Min die.
**Green (pick 1):** Attune (A) — Boost yourself, persistent+exclusive; damage from that bonus becomes [energy/element]. Extrasensory Awareness (R) — Defend vs. zone-changing damage by rolling [quality].

### 18 — Unknown
*"You don't know the source of your powers: they either just manifested one day, or hint at a bigger mystery."*
**Yellow (pick 2):** Brainstorm (A) — Attack one target Min die, another Mid die, Boost Max die. Strange Enhancement (A) — Boost all nearby allies Max+Mid, Hinder yourself Min die. Volatile Creations (R) — when a bonus/penalty/creation of yours is destroyed, deal damage equal to a [power] die roll.
**Special:** gain a Social quality instead of a Green ability.

### 19 — Higher Power
*"You have been chosen by a higher force or are a being from another realm. Your powers are a reflection of your calling or true form."*
**Yellow (pick 2):** Command Power (R) — redirect [element] damage taken to another target. Dangerous Explosion (A) — Attack multiple targets Mid die, Hinder all hit Min die, Hinder yourself Max die. Embolden (A) — Attack, Boost nearby heroes taking two chosen basic actions Min die until next turn. Resolve (A) — Boost yourself, then remove a penalty or Recover Min die.
**Green (pick 1):** Resilience (I) — remove -1 penalties at the start of your turn. Twist Reality (R) — take 1 irreducible damage to reroll entire pool after rolling.

### 20 — The Multiverse
*"You have traveled or been flung through the realms of time and space. Without The Multiverse itself, you would not be who you are now."*
**Yellow (pick 2):** Power From Beyond (A) — Boost yourself Max die, persistent+exclusive, then Attack Min die. Respond In Kind (R) — close-range attacker also takes damage equal to their effect die. Dread Pallor (A) — Hinder multiple targets, Mid die one, Min die the rest. Reality Scorned (A) — Attack; if target survives, Hinder Max die.
**Special:** gain one extra power from any category, instead of a Green ability.
