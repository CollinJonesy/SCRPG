# Red Abilities — Complete Reference (Step 5)
Source: printed pg.106–111 (PDF pg.114–119).

## How it works — the gating mechanic, confirmed verbatim
"Choose two Red abilities. The Red abilities are grouped by power and quality category, but not just for convenience. **You must have a die assigned to a power or quality in a category to use with an ability from that category.** If an ability doesn't use a power or quality at all (like most Inherent abilities), **you must have a power or quality rated d6 or higher in that category** in order to take it. Some Red abilities also specify that they can only be used with a specific power or quality instead of any from within that category."

**This is the direct, confirmed answer to the gating question flagged back in HeroCreation_Process.md:** Red Abilities are locked behind category ownership. A hero can only select a Red Ability from a category (Athletic, Elemental/Energy, Psychic, etc.) if they actually have a Power or Quality in that category on their sheet. Abilities with no power/quality slot (mostly Inherent-type) instead require a d6+ rated power/quality in that category to unlock. A few abilities go further and name one *specific* power/quality rather than "any in the category."

You choose **two** Red Abilities total, from any categories you qualify for (they don't need to be the same category as each other).

## Worked Example — Time-Slinger's Red Abilities (continuing the running example)
"Skimming the list of Red abilities and the categories Jim has access to, Christopher settles on **Give Time** from Psychic powers and **Final Wrath** from Mental qualities."

**Give Time** needs a Psychic power and gets renamed **"Temporal Bootstrap"**:
> Boost another hero using Postcognition. If that hero has already acted for the turn, use your Max die, and that hero loses Health equal to your Min die. That hero acts next in the action order.

**Final Wrath** gets renamed **"Showdown"**:
> Attack using Self-Discipline. Use your Max+Mid+Min dice. Take a major twist.

*(Note: this confirms Self-Discipline was assigned as a Mental quality earlier in the build — consistent with Personality.md's worked example.)*
