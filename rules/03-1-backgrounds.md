# Backgrounds — Complete Reference
Source: printed pg.48–54 (PDF pg.56–62). Transcribed directly from clean screenshots — high confidence.

## How it works
Roll two dice (die sizes depend on what step you're on — for a fresh hero, this is the very first roll of character creation). Using **either single die's value, or the sum of both**, select a Background number 1–20 from the chart below. That Background grants:
- Dice to assign to specific Qualities (2 or 3, depending on entry)
- A Principle (from the listed category — see page ranges below)
- A new set of dice to roll for the next step, Power Source

## Full Entries

### 1 — Upper Class
Assign dice to two of: **Fitness**, **Persuasion**, *or* select from **Mental** qualities category.
Choose a **Responsibility** principle (pages 138–141). Roll for power source selection.
> *Flavor:* "You were born in the upper echelons of high society. Likely you're also pretty darn rich."

### 2 — Blank Slate
Assign dice to two of: select from **Mental** qualities category, select from **Physical** qualities category.
Choose an **Identity** principle (pages 135–137). Roll for power source selection.
> *Flavor:* "You remember nothing. Were you brainwashed? Maybe you were just created? One way or another, you have no history. You start now. Your future is what you make of it."

### 3 — Struggling
Assign dice to **three** of: **Banter**, **Criminal Underworld Info**, *or* select from **Physical** qualities category.
Choose a **Responsibility** principle (pages 138–141). Roll for power source selection.
> *Flavor:* "You've been down and out. Perhaps you've recovered a bit, but you also might still be stuck in a terrible situation. Low on resources and luck, you did your best, but it often just wasn't good enough."

### 4 — Adventurer
Assign dice to two of: **History**, **Leadership**, *or* select from **Physical** qualities category.
Choose an **Expertise** principle (pages 127–130). Roll for power source selection.
> *Flavor:* "In your past, you have sought excitement and adventure at every turn. Even before you were a hero, you were a thrill seeker."

### 5 — Unremarkable
Assign dice to two of: **Close Combat**, select from **Mental** qualities category, select from **Social** qualities category.
Choose an **Identity** principle (pages 135–137). Roll for power source selection.
> *Flavor:* "You were just a regular person, leading a normal life, until something came along and changed your life in a major way. You came from a commonplace background, but now you're a hero."

### 6 — Law Enforcement
Assign dice to two of: **Close Combat**, **Criminal Underworld Info**, **Ranged Combat**, select from **Mental** qualities category, select from **Social** qualities category.
Choose a **Responsibility** principle (pages 138–141). Roll for power source selection.
> *Flavor:* "You're a member of law enforcement, such as a beat cop, detective, or perhaps a lawyer or judge. You made a career of the law at one point. Perhaps you still do."

### 7 — Academic
Assign dice to two of: **Leadership**, **Self-Discipline**, select from **Information** qualities category.
Choose an **Expertise** principle (pages 127–130). Roll for power source selection.
> *Flavor:* "You work or study in a field of knowledge. You could be a school teacher, professor, researcher, or clergy member. The pursuit of knowledge is very important to you, and possibly what led you to become a hero."

### 8 — Tragic
Assign dice to two of: **Banter**, **Close Combat**, **Imposing**, select from **Mental** qualities category.
Choose an **Ideals** principle (pages 131–134). Roll for power source selection.
> *Flavor:* "Your history is eclipsed by a major negative event that shaped the rest of your life. You struggle to overcome the memory of the tragic event, be it the loss of a loved one or something that happened to you directly. Either way, the tragedy both fuels and haunts you."

### 9 — Performer
Assign dice to two of: **Acrobatics**, **Creativity**, **Finesse**, select from **Social** qualities category.
Choose a **Responsibility** principle (pages 138–141). Roll for power source selection.
> *Flavor:* "You were born for the stage. How you present yourself to the world is important to you, whether in or out of the limelight."

### 10 — Military
Assign dice to two of: **Leadership**, **Self-Discipline**, select from **Physical** qualities category.
Choose an **Ideals** principle (pages 131–134). Roll for power source selection.
> *Flavor:* "You have some sort of combat training, possibly as part of an organized armed forces. You might have even had combat experience before you became a hero."

### 11 — Retired
Assign dice to two of: select from **Information** qualities category, select from **Social** qualities category.
Choose an **Identity** principle (pages 135–137). Roll for power source selection. *(Note: this entry rolls a d12 + two smaller dice for power source, per the chart — larger than most.)*
> *Flavor:* "You used to wear the cape and cowl, but hung them up long ago. Now, something has changed, making you feel compelled to once again take up the fight for what is right. You never thought you would be here again."

### 12 — Criminal
Assign dice to two of: **Criminal Underworld Info**, **Imposing**, select from **Physical** qualities category.
Choose an **Expertise** principle (pages 127–130). Roll for power source selection.
> *Flavor:* "You spent too much time on the wrong side of the law, but something changed for you. Now you've turned over a new leaf, using your powers and abilities to be the best hero you can."

### 13 — Medical
Assign one die to **Medicine (Required)**, and the other two dice to two of: **Finesse**, **Science**, **Technology**, select from **Mental** qualities category.
Choose an **Expertise** principle (pages 127–130). Roll for power source selection.
> *Flavor:* "You were in the business of healing, as a doctor or nurse or maybe even a veterinarian. Given your medical background, you have a lot of experience with treating injuries and diseases."

### 14 — Anachronistic
Assign dice to two of: **History**, **Magical Lore**, **Technology**, select from **Physical** qualities category.
Choose an **Esoteric** principle (pages 124–126). Roll for power source selection.
> *Flavor:* "One way or another, you aren't quite in your time. You could be a stranded time traveler, or just a person who fits more with the ideals and customs of a time long before or after the one in which you currently reside. Either way, though this time is not your own, you still fight to protect it."

### 15 — Exile
Assign dice to two of: **Conviction**, **Insight**, select from **Information** qualities category.
Choose an **Ideals** principle (pages 131–134). Roll for power source selection.
> *Flavor:* "You're far from your home, one way or another. You may have left of your own accord, but it's equally likely that you were sent away from whatever place you came from. Either way, you're making your own way in the land where you now live."

### 16 — Former Villain
Assign dice to two of: **Conviction**, select from **Information** qualities category, select from **Social** qualities category.
Choose an **Expertise** principle (pages 127–130). Roll for power source selection.
> *Flavor:* "You used to be a foe to the heroes, but you've changed your stripes. You may have realized the evil of your former ways, or your motivation might have changed. To all you with those you once fought, you are now a hero, though many other heroes are hesitant to trust you."

### 17 — Interstellar
Assign dice (first die is a d12) to two of: select from **Information** qualities category, select from **Mental** qualities category.
Choose an **Esoteric** principle (pages 124–126). Roll for power source selection.
> *Flavor:* "You come from beyond the stars! As a newcomer to planet Earth, you may be unaware of strange customs here, but you can still communicate with Earthlings, one way or another. You might be an alien, or a human from a civilization lost in space long ago, or something else entirely."

### 18 — Dynasty
Assign dice to two of: **Close Combat**, **Fitness**, **History**, select from **Social** qualities category.
Choose an **Ideals** principle (pages 131–134). Roll for power source selection.
> *Flavor:* "You come from a line of heroes. It could be that your parents and their parents have all been heroes. Maybe you're adopted into a hero family. Regardless of how you came to be a part of this dynasty, heroism is part of your life."

### 19 — Otherworldly
Assign dice to two of: **Magical Lore**, **Otherworldly Mythos**, select from **Mental** qualities category.
Choose an **Esoteric** principle (pages 124–126). Roll for power source selection.
> *Flavor:* "You have at least a little of the uncanny in you. You could be a fully supernatural creature, or perhaps the spawn of one and a human."

### 20 — Created
Assign one die (d12) and one other die to two of: **Alertness**, **Science**, **Technology**, select from **Physical** qualities category.
Choose an **Expertise** principle (pages 127–130). Roll for power source selection.
> *Flavor:* "Were you built to be the hero you are today? Perhaps. But it's undeniable that you were created by someone or something. As a constructed being, you don't have the same experiences or expectations as similar organic creatures, but you still feel drawn to the role of hero."

## Worked example (from the book, hero "Time-Slinger" f.k.a. Chrono-Ranger)
Christopher rolls two dice and gets 6 and 8. Background 8 is Tragic, which doesn't fit the hero's story. Background 6 is Law Enforcement (Jim was a sheriff before time-hopping) — decent fit. But **adding 6+8 = 14, Anachronistic** — since Jim is a man out of time, this fits perfectly, so that's what's selected. **This confirms the selection rule concretely: single die value OR the sum of both dice, GM/player's choice based on best narrative fit.**

Following through Anachronistic (#14): Christopher assigns dice to **History** and **Ranged Combat** (from the Physical category, for Jim's gunslinging past), records those on the Qualities section. He picks the Esoteric principle **"Principle of the Time Traveler."** He then rolls for power source using the dice Anachronistic grants (shown as d10, d8, d6 in the example art).
