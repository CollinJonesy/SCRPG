# Storm Imps

## Description

Made from bits and pieces of whatever the Storm pulled into its Rifts, deformed beyond recognition into horrid clawed and winged vaguely humanoid monstrosities. They y all over the place, seemingly immune to the pulls of the Storm, ripping chunks of whatever they can get their claws into and throwing them back into the Rifts they came out of.

## Abilities

### [A] [None] "Internal Storm"
When Storm Imps Attack, they can convert 1 point of damage to a persistent and exclusive penalty.
