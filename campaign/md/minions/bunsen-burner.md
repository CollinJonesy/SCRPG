# Bunsen Burner

## Description

Bunsen Burner used to be a minor villain. He underwent a series of invasive experiments from the EMP and recently broke out of their facility, hiding in the Drowned. He's got ripped arms that are good for punching, but also are on fire. No literally, his arms are always on fire.

## Abilities

### [A] [None] "Unstable Ignition"
Whenever Bunsen Burner Attacks, roll his die twice and use the higher result. If he rolled the same number twice, reduce his die size by one.

### [A] [None] "Firewall"
Whenever Bunsen Burner succeeds at his save, use that roll as an Attack against his attacker.

## Tactics

Bunsen Burner gets into the thick of combat, swinging his arms of fire in an attempt to ignite as many opponents as possible.
