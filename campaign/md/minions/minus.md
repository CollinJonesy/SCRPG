# Minus

## Description

The Pairing of Plus & Minus really powers up the criminal activity of the Shockers. They are wearing their blue colors, with "Plus and Minus" cutouts (holes in their shirts).

## Abilities

### [A] [None] "Negative Polarity"
Minus gets a +2 to Hinder Actions.

## Tactics

Minus is the brother of Plus. Minus is never Negative with his brother around, but he's quick to Hinder those who get in his brother's way.
