# Plus

## Description

The Pairing of Plus & Minus really powers up the criminal activity of the Shockers. They are wearing their blue colors, with "Plus and Minus" cutouts (holes in their shirts).

## Abilities

### [A] [None] "Positive Polarity"
Plus gets a +2 to Boost Actions.

## Tactics

Plus is the brother of Minus. Plus always tries to be Positive around his brother, so he [Boosts] him up whenever the opportunity arises.
