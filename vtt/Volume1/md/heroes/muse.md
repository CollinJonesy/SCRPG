# Muse

## Out

Boost an ally using Insight.

## Abilities

### [A] "Mental Block"
Attack using Suggestion. Hinder the target using your Min die.

### [A] "Your Best Selves"
Boost using Telepathy. Apply that bonus to all hero Attack and Overcome actions until your next turn.

### [A] "Principle of Mastery"
Overcome in a situation that uses your powers in a new way. Use your Max die. You and each of your allies gain a hero point.

### [A] "Principle of the Inner Demon"
Overcome a challenge by tapping into your dark psyche. Use your Max die. You and each of your allies gain a hero point.

### [A] "Conjured Nightmare"
Hinder multiple targets using Illusions. Use your Mid die for one and your Min die for the rest.

### [A] "Open Your Inner Eye"
Boost yourself using Intuition. Use your Max die. That Bonus is persistent and exclusive. Attack using your Min die.

### [A] "Overwhelmed Mind"
Attack a minion using Suggestion. If that minion would be removed, instead you control its next action and then remove it. Otherwise, Hinder it with your Min die.

### [A] "Twist Your Mind"
Attack using Telepathy. Use your Max die. Hinder the target with your Min die. That penalty is persistent.

### [A] "Debilitate"
Hinder using Suggestion. Use your Max+Mid dice. Damage yourself equal to your Min die. Then if you rolled doubles, Attack the target with your Mid+Min dice.

### [I] "Perfect Intuition"
As long as you have at least one bonus created from Insight, treat Suggestion as one size higher (max d12).

### [A] "Shared Terror"
Attack using Conviction. Use your Max+Mid+Min dice. Take a major twist.

### [A] "Out Ability"
Boost an ally using Insight.
