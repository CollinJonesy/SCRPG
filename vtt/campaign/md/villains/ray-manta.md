# Ray Manta

## Abilities

### [I] [Other] "Contingencies Upon Contingencies"
Whenever you would be reduced to 0 or fewer Health, prevent that damage and reduce all your power dice by one size. If this reduces any dice to below a d4, you are knocked out.

### [A] [Hinder] "Prepared For Anything"
Boost using Inventions. Use your Max die. Hinder using your Mid die. Attack using your Min die.

### [A] [Boost] "Ramshackle Creation"
Boost using Inventions and use your Max die, also Boost with your Mid die, and either make one of those bonuses persistent and exclusive or Attack with your Min die.

### [R] [Defend] "Tinfoil Components"
Discard one of your bonuses to Defend against all Attacks against you until your next turn, using that bonus value as the Defend result.

### [A] [Attack] "Unexpected Accuracy"
Attack using Gadgets. If you roll doubles, add that value to your Attack. If you roll triples, add all three dice to your Attack.

## Upgrades

### [I] [Recover] "The Manta Body"
Ray Manta built a bigger, semi-autonomous suit for himself, represented as a d4 lieutenant with the Distance Attack, Escape Plan, Reliable, and Recovery abilities from page 236. (When using this upgrade, Ray Manta has 15 additional Health.)

## Mastery

### [I] [Overcome] "Master of Mad Science"
As long as you have access to materials, you can automatically succeed when Overcoming a challenge by using scientific principles and inventions.
