# Moderating (Running the Game)


The GM in SCRPG is a **writer and scene-shaper**, not a rules arbiter. The system pushes you to keep the
fiction moving and let heroism (and failure) be interesting.


## Scene structure
SCRPG features **three** distinct scene types — **Action**, **Social**, and **Montage** (montage is a
separate category, not a sub-type of social):
- **Action Scenes** — conflict, dice pools, GYRO shifts.
- **Social Scenes** — dramatic interaction among characters.
- **Montage Scenes** — the connective tissue of a story (travel, training, recovery); "the glue that
  holds the narrative together."
A session is usually one or more *Issues*; a campaign arc is a **Collection** (the book does not use
"Volume" for this — that term refers to published comic volumes).


- **Action Scene flow:** Set the scene → heroes and villains act in an order → environment/minion twists
  escalate → someone drops in status or the objective is met → scene ends.
- **Scene Tracker:** many scenes use a tracker (a progress bar from START to END). Environment *twists*
  and *threats* unlock as the tracker advances; if it hits END with the goal unmet, the villains win the
  scene. The tracker is zone-based (Green → Yellow → Red — the **Scene GYRO**), not just a generic bar.


## The GM's role
The book frames the GM's work around **portraying the world, running villains & minions, and triggering
environment twists** — it does *not* use a formalized "5 jobs" checklist, nor does it have a "set a target
number / TN" mechanic (action resolution is GYRO/dice-pool based; there are no target numbers to beat).
Hero Points are covered in Chapter 2, not here.


## Environment twists
Every scene has an **Environment** with twists. Two distinct twist systems exist in the book and should
not be conflated:
- **Action-result twists (Minor vs Major):** these are *outcome tiers* of a roll, not color zones. Per the
  result table, a roll of 1–3 = "fail, or succeed with a **Major Twist**"; 4–7 = "succeed with a **Minor
  Twist**"; 8–11 = succeed (no twist); 12+ = "succeed with a Minor Twist." So Minor/Major describe how
  *complicated* a success/failure is — they are not tied to Green/Yellow/Red.
- **Environment (Scene) Twists:** each environment has its own **Minor and Major twists listed under every
  Scene GYRO zone — Green, Yellow, *and* Red** (not Major-only-in-Red). They unlock as the Scene Tracker
  advances. Example environments: Argaeus Tower, Megalopolis, Freedom Plaza, Wagner II Mars Base.


## Damage & status in play
- Villains and minions use their **status die** (set by their "Number of Minions" / scene status table).
- Heroes shift Green→Yellow→Red→Out as they take damage (see [[03_GYRO_Status]]).
- A **damage save** can blunt a shift.
