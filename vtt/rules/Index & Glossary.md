# Index & Glossary
# A
## Abilities
*pages 12, 15, 19, 22, 44-45, 154*

Tricks, tactics, or talents used in scenes by heroes and villains. For heroes, abilities fall into Green, Yellow, Red, or [[Index_and_Glossary#Out|Out]] zones. Villains have access to all their abilities, regardless of zone. [[Index_and_Glossary#Abilities|Abilities]] modify and combine basic actions in many ways to change how dice are applied and produce unique effects.

## Action
*pages 12, 17, 18-23, 24-29*

A thing a character does on their turn. An ability that takes an action to perform uses all of that turn.

## Action Order
*pages 17-23, 164-165*

The elective initiative system used in SCRPG. Also, a term for the order in which everyone acts in a scene, which is determined one turn at a time over the course of a round until everyone has acted.

## Action Scenes
*pages 9, 15, 159, 161, 184*

Combat or action oriented scenes, for brawls, chases, and daring rescues.

## Adventure Issue
*pages 146, 252, 253-266, 267-289*

Published SCRPG playable issues, such as the two in this book in Chapter 6.

## Alternate Rewards
*pages 248-249*

Optional rewards to replace the standard hero point bonuses that a GM can choose to offer.

## Archetype
*pages 46, 72-99*

A characteristic used during hero creation. How you use your powers and your role in a team of other heroes. A hero’s archetype determines some of their powers and qualities, some of their abilities, and their second principle.

## Attack
*pages 19, 24*

An action used to deal damage.

## Auxiliary Sheet
*pages 12, 45, 443*

A sheet secondary to the hero sheet, for holding additional information in the case of heroes with more complicated builds.

# B
## Back Issues
*pages 10, 142*

Whenever players finish an issue of play, they give the issue a name and number and add it to the [[Index_and_Glossary#Back Issues|Back Issues]] section of their hero sheet.

## Background
*pages 46, 49-54*

A characteristic used during hero creation. Where your hero came from before they became a hero. A hero’s background determines some of their qualities and their first principle.

## Basic Actions
*pages 19, 29, 154*

The six fundamental types of actions that characters in this game take. See [[Index_and_Glossary#Attack|Attack]], [[Index_and_Glossary#Overcome|Overcome]], [[Index_and_Glossary#Boost|Boost]], [[Index_and_Glossary#Hinder|Hinder]], [[Index_and_Glossary#Defend|Defend]], and the special [[Index_and_Glossary#Recover|Recover]] action. Basic actions can be modified by and used as part of abilities.

## Bonus
*pages 26-27*

A positive mod representing favorable circumstances that increases the value of an effect die. If not persistent, it goes away after one usage.

## Boost
*pages 19, 26-27*

An action used to help yourself or another character, resulting in a bonus.

## Bystanders
*pages 148*

[[Index_and_Glossary#NPCs|NPCs]] that are not specifically foes to the heroes, nor notable allies. The people in the background or on the sidelines. Frequently the people in danger from the villains in the scene.

# C
## Challenges
*pages 25, 160-164, 189-199*

Obstacles, dangers to [[Index_and_Glossary#NPCs|NPCs]], or complications that must be dealt with in the timeframe of the ongoing scene, most commonly with [[Index_and_Glossary#Overcome|Overcome]] actions. There are six types of challenges: Simple (page 161), Linear (page 162), Multiple Solutions (page 162), Branching Outcomes (page 163), Timed (page 164), and Doomsday Devices (page 164).

## Collection
*pages 10, 32, 142, 249*

When a hero player has 6 [[Index_and_Glossary#Back Issues|Back Issues]] on their hero sheet, they get erased from the Back [[Index_and_Glossary#Issue|Issue]] section, gathered into a [[Index_and_Glossary#Collection|Collection]], and recorded on their hero sheet. Collections can be called upon once each issue to maximize a die, negate a twist, or add to the story in a scene.

## Constructed Method
*pages 42*

Another method of hero creation, following the process of the [[Index_and_Glossary#Guided Method|Guided Method]], but ignoring the dice rolling in favor of choosing whatever you want for your hero in each category.

# D
## Damage
*pages 19, 24*

[[Index_and_Glossary#Damage|Damage]] is dealt as the result of an [[Index_and_Glossary#Attack|Attack]] action. [[Index_and_Glossary#Damage|Damage]] either reduces health, or is rolled against with a damage save, depending on the type of target being dealt damage.

## Damage Save
*pages 17*

When something represented by a single die, like a minion or a lieutenant, is attacked, it rolls its die as a save against that damage. The results of the save vary by what type of character it is. ## Dice Pool

# G
## Green Zone
*pages 12-13, 147*

The highest band of [[Index_and_Glossary#GYRO|GYRO]]. A hero has access to their Green zone abilities when their status is anything other than [[Index_and_Glossary#Out|Out]].

## Guided Method
*pages 42, 46*

The primary method of hero creation described in Chapter 3. A way to build a hero in this game that involves a lot of choices, guided by rolling dice to move from step to step to determine the building blocks of your hero.

*pages 14, 16, 147*

An abbreviation of the four zones of the game: Green, Yellow, Red, and [[Index_and_Glossary#Out|Out]]. # H
## Health
*pages 14, 113, 239*

A measurement of the physical health, composure, and fatigue of a hero or villain.

## Health Range
*pages 12-13, 113, 239*

The ranges of [[Index_and_Glossary#Health|Health]] that indicate a character’s current personal zone.

## Hero
*pages 2-3, 8, 41-143, 179, 181, 292-345*

The main characters played by non-GM players, whether created using Chapter 3 or premade, such as the heroes found in Chapter 7.

## Hero Advancement
*pages 142-143*

The process by which heroes grow and change over the course of many adventures and issues.

## Hero Creation
*pages 41-141*

The process of making your own [[Index_and_Glossary#Sentinel Comics|Sentinel Comics]] RPG hero! All of Chapter 3 is devoted to this. See [[Index_and_Glossary#Guided Method|Guided Method]] and [[Index_and_Glossary#Constructed Method|Constructed Method]].

## Hero Points
*pages 14, 31*

Points earned by heroes in a variety of ways, notably for playing to their principles in scenes and through roleplaying. A maximum of 5 hero points can be earned by each hero over the course of one issue of play. [[Index_and_Glossary#Hero|Hero]] points from a previous issue of play are spent at the start of an issue to gain hero point bonuses.

## Hero Point Bonus
*pages 31*

Bonuses gained by heroes at the start of an issue by spending the hero points they earned last issue. [[Index_and_Glossary#Hero|Hero]] point bonuses are floating bonuses that can be invoked during the issue they are gained.

# L
## Last Stand
*pages 17, 149*

When a minion is at a , its damage save behavior changes and it doesn’t degrade any further. When a minion successfully saves against damage, it survives the attack. [[Index_and_Glossary#Damage|Damage]] that beats the value rolled by a minion defeats that minion, removing it from the scene.

## Lieutenants
*pages 17, 149, 156, 160, 207, 402-417*

Powerful [[Index_and_Glossary#NPCs|NPCs]], each represented by a single die. Usually used as a major opponent of the heroes. Their damage saves are much hardier than the damage saves of minions, but they also have a rule for massive damage on page 149.

## Location
*pages 16, 158, 160*

A specific place within an environment that can involve a bit of complexity in its thematic layout.

# M
## Major Twists
*pages 25, 29-30, 155*

Twists that create very notable and/or long-lasting problems or introduce major threats to the scene.

## Max Die
*pages 21*

When rolling a dice pool, the die that rolled the highest number is the Max die.

## Mid Die
*pages 21*

When rolling a dice pool, the die that rolled the middle number is the Mid die.

## Min Die
*pages 21*

When rolling a dice pool, the die that rolled the lowest number is the Min die. Minions.......... 17, 148-149, 156, 160, 166, 204-206, 402-417 Standard [[Index_and_Glossary#NPCs|NPCs]], each represented by a single die. Usually used as opponents of the heroes. Most effective in groups.

## Minor Twists
*pages 25, 29-30, 154*

Twists that create unexpected wrinkles or introduce minor threats to the scene.

## Mods
*pages 22, 26-27, 160*

Bonuses and penalties generated with the [[Index_and_Glossary#Boost|Boost]] or [[Index_and_Glossary#Hinder|Hinder]] actions.

## Montage Scenes
*pages 9, 32-33, 159, 171*

Narrative focused scenes, for travel, recovery, repair, investigation, etc.

## Movement
*pages 168-169*

If a hero wants to move within the same location, they can do so without an action. If a hero wants to move from one location to another, they spend their whole turn doing so, usually, though they can perform a [[Index_and_Glossary#Boost|Boost]], [[Index_and_Glossary#Hinder|Hinder]], or [[Index_and_Glossary#Defend|Defend]] action as well.

# N
## NPCs
*pages 8, 148-156*

All characters not controlled by hero players, including villains, minions, lieutenants, environment threats, allies, innocent bystanders, and anyone else controlled by the GM.

# O
## Out
*pages 12-13, 14, 101-104, 147*

The end of [[Index_and_Glossary#GYRO|GYRO]]. When [[Index_and_Glossary#Out|Out]], a hero only has access to their [[Index_and_Glossary#Out|Out]] ability. When the scene tracker goes to [[Index_and_Glossary#Out|Out]], the scene ends immediately and catastrophically.

## Overcome
*pages 19, 25, 154, 169*

An action used to get past an obstacle.

# P
## Penalty
*pages 26-27*

A negative mod that reduces the value of an effect die. If not persistent, it goes away after one usage.

## Persistent Mods
*pages 26*

[[Index_and_Glossary#Mods|Mods]] that last until the end of the scene, or until they are removed by taking action against them or some other effect.

## Personal Status
*pages 12, 147*

A character’s status, based on [[Index_and_Glossary#Health|Health]] or other factors in the case of some villains. A character’s personal status can be different from the scene or environment’s status. In those cases, use the status that is closer to [[Index_and_Glossary#Out|Out]].

## Personality
*pages 46, 100-105*

A characteristic used during hero creation. Your general demeanor and how you react when under pressure. A hero’s personality determines their [[Index_and_Glossary#Out|Out]] ability and status dice, and also grants them a unique quality. ## Plot Characters
*pages 150*

Named [[Index_and_Glossary#NPCs|NPCs]] that play significant roles to advance a story but don’t always act in action scenes.

## Powers
*pages 15, 20, 47, 115-118*

Talents, whether innate, coming from gear or gadgets, or some combination of the two. [[Index_and_Glossary#Powers|Powers]] are stats for both heroes and villains, rated as die sizes from to .

## Power Source
*pages 46, 56-71*

A characteristic used during hero creation. What changed you into a hero and what fuels your powers. A hero’s power source determines some of their powers and qualities and some of their abilities.

## Principles
*pages 14, 45, 122-141*

Representations of a hero’s core beliefs and fundamental personality traits. [[Index_and_Glossary#Principles|Principles]] give heroes a roleplaying prompt, as well as minor and major twist questions, and a green zone ability that generates hero points. [[Index_and_Glossary#Principles|Principles]] fall into five categories: Esoteric (pages 124-126), Expertise (pages 127-130), Ideals (pages 131-134), Identity (pages 135-137), and Responsibility (pages 138- 141).

# Q
## Qualities
*pages 15, 20, 47, 119-121*

Learned traits and skills acquired through training, education, mentoring, etc. [[Index_and_Glossary#Qualities|Qualities]] are stats for both heroes and villains, rated as die sizes from  to .

# R
## Reaction
*pages 12, 29, 45*

A thing you can do in reaction to a trigger. Each character can perform only one reaction per round, gaining it back at the start of their turn, unless they have an ability stating otherwise.

## Recover
*pages 19, 29, 32-33, 171*

A special action used by abilities or in [[Index_and_Glossary#Montage Scenes|Montage Scenes]] to get [[Index_and_Glossary#Health|Health]] back.

## Red Zone
*pages 12-13, 106-111, 147*

The lowest band of [[Index_and_Glossary#GYRO|GYRO]]. Red abilities can only be used when the hero’s personal or scene status is in the Red zone.

## Results
*pages 17, 21-22*

The numbers rolled on dice, either individually or as part of a dice pool. [[Index_and_Glossary#Results|Results]] can be affected by mods.

## Retcon
*pages 112*

One of the last steps of hero creation, allowing you to go back and tweak specific mechanical aspects of your hero.

## Risky Action
*pages 19*

A way to add an extra effect to a basic action by invoking a minor twist. Only works on a basic action, not on abilities.

## Round
*pages 15, 23*

A cycle of play over which every character and element in a scene has a chance to act on their turn. Once each actor in the scene has taken their turn, the round is over and a new round begins.

# S
## Scene
*pages 9, 159-160*

The sections of play of the game, falling into three categories, depending on the type of roleplaying happening. See [[Index_and_Glossary#Action|Action]] [[Index_and_Glossary#Scene|Scene]], Social [[Index_and_Glossary#Scene|Scene]], and Montage [[Index_and_Glossary#Scene|Scene]].

## Scene Difficulty
*pages 185-188*

Scenes can be built as easy, moderate, or difficult, depending on the number and severity of threats and challenges.

## Scene Elements
*pages 146*

The pieces of a scene that work together to create the playable game.

## Scene GYRO
*pages 16, 147*

The particular status of the scene itself. Spaces on the scene tracker are checked off as the scene progresses, moving from Green to Yellow to Red. If the scene tracker runs out of checkable spaces, the scene goes to [[Index_and_Glossary#Out|Out]] and ends in some catastrophic fashion.

## Scene Tracker
*pages 15, 16, 23, 147-148, 159, 188*

A series of spaces broken up into Green, Yellow, and Red zones that measures the tension and danger in the scene.

## Sentinel Comics
*pages 2, 291-431*

The (fake) comic book company that prints such iconic titles as Justice Comics, Mystery Comics, Sentinels of Freedom, and more! [[Index_and_Glossary#Sentinel Comics|Sentinel Comics]] is the name of the IP of this game, as well as many others.

## Social Scenes
*pages 9, 34-35, 159, 169-170, 184*

Interpersonal focused scenes, for dramatic and interesting interactions among characters, whether heroes or [[Index_and_Glossary#NPCs|NPCs]].

## Status
*pages 14, 16, 20*

A stat that changes based on health for heroes and some villains, notable factors for other villains, and can be affected by the scene itself, based on the scene tracker. The four zones of status are Green, Yellow, Red, and [[Index_and_Glossary#Out|Out]]. Index & Glossary  438

## Status Dice
*pages 12-13, 101-105*

The dice assigned to each zone for a hero or a villain with status based on [[Index_and_Glossary#Health|Health]], chosen during creation of that character. Rolled as part of a dice pool.

# T

Hostile minions, lieutenants, and villains. Introduced by the scene and by the environment.

## Turn

The times that characters or other elements act in a scene. Heroes have turns, but so do minions, lieutenants, villains, environments, and even the scene itself. A turn tends to represent around 1 to 3 panels of a comic book. The primary antagonists of the game. Villains have names, powers, qualities, a factor that determines their status, and abilities. They may also have upgrades and masteries.

## Villain Approaches
*pages 152, 208-219*

A characteristic used during the process of villain creation. The way a villain takes on obstacles.

## Villain Archetypes
*pages 152, 220-234*

A characteristic used during the process of villain creation. The sort of villain a particular villain is.

## Villain Masteries
*pages 149, 238*

An extra ability that upgraded villains have that allows them to always succeed at a specific type of [[Index_and_Glossary#Overcome|Overcome]] action.

## Villain Sheet

The place where the mechanics a GM needs to run a villain are found.

## Villain Upgrades
*pages 149, 235-238*

Additional abilities and elements in a villain’s stats that make them more powerful or give them more options.

# X
## X-Card
*pages 176*

A safety tool to protect all players at the table.

# Y
## Yellow Zone
*pages 12-13, 147*

The middle band of [[Index_and_Glossary#GYRO|GYRO]]. Yellow abilities can only be used when the hero’s personal or scene status is in either the Yellow or Red zone.

# Z
## Zones
*pages 14*

An abstract representation of how dire a situation is, for a character personally or for the scene as a whole. Being in a specific zone has different mechanical effects, from the status dice a hero uses to what abilities they have access to. The four zones are Green, Yellow, Red, and Out.
