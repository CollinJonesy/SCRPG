# Glossary


Quick reference for SCRPG terms and shorthand used throughout the wiki and the rulebook reconstruction.


## Dice & rolls
- **Die ladder:** 🎲d4 < 🎲d6 < 🎲d8 < 🎲d10 < 🎲d12.
- **Pool** — the dice you roll for an action (1 Power + 1 Quality + status die + bonuses). The **effect die** is the **Mid die** by default (unless an ability or effect says otherwise); once dice are on the table, the *rolled number* decides Min/Mid/Max — die size does not override the number (a d12 can roll low and be the Min result).
- **Min / Mid / Max die** — the lowest / middle / highest die by **rolled number** (not by die size). Used by abilities to scale effects.
- **Penalty** — a *numeric* mod that reduces a result (created by a Hinder); not a separate die added to the pool.
- **Degrade** — drop one step on the ladder (🎲d12→🎲d10→…→🎲d4). Floor is 🎲d4.


## Status
- **GYRO** — Green → Yellow → Red → Out status track. Gate for which abilities you can use.
- **Status die** — the die you add to every pool, set by your current GYRO zone (shrinks as you get hurt).
- **Health** — a measurement of a character's physical health, composure, and fatigue.
- **Health Range** — the threshold numbers that determine which GYRO zone (Green/Yellow/Red) a character's status is in; you shift zones when the effect die's rolled number crosses these thresholds.
- **Damage save** — a roll used by **minions/lieutenants** (single-die NPCs) to blunt or avoid a status drop; heroes take Health damage directly, they do not make a "damage save."


## Character pieces
- **Power** — a talent a hero can use to do things (Flight, Energy Blasts, Telepathy), whether it comes from a supernatural source, genetics, or "just old fashioned training." Granted mostly by Power Source.
- **Quality** — who you are (Athletic, any Social). Broad; usable for many actions. Has a die.
- **Background** — Step 1; upbringing/role; grants Qualities + Principle Category + dice.
- **Power Source** — Step 2; cosmic origin; grants Powers + selection dice. (20 sources.)
- **Archetype** — Step 3; role template; grants Default mode + Principle type. (20 archetypes.)
- **Personality** — Step 4; temperament; sets GYRO status dice. (20 personalities.)
- **Red Abilities** — Step 5; your desperation moves (chosen from a shared list).
- **Principles** — roleplaying prompts that grant a hero-point-generating ability; tied to one of **five** categories: Identity, Expertise, Responsibility, Ideals, Esoteric.


## Scene & game terms
- **Issue** — a single scenario/adventure (roughly a session of play, though a long issue can span more than one session).
- **Collection** — the book's term for a multi-Issue story arc (a "collection happens every six issues").
- **H** — the number of heroes **in the scene** (the H icon).
- **Scene Tracker** — a progress bar (START→END); environment twists unlock as it advances.
- **Environment Twist** — a hazard/escalation. Every environment has a **Major Twist** listed under **each** zone (Green, Yellow, and Red), plus minor twists.
- **Hero Point** — currency to reroll, boost, or refuse a status drop.
- **Minion / Lieutenant** — villain threat mass (see [[11_Minions_and_Lieutenants]]).
- **Number of Minions table** — maps current minion count → villain status die.
- **Modular mode** — a swappable ability loadout chosen between Issues.


## Abbreviations
- **GYRO** = Green/Yellow/Red/Out.
- **P / Q** = Power / Quality.
- **ATK/DEF/BOO/HIN/REC/OVC** = Attack/Defend/Boost/Hinder/Recover/Overcome (the six action icons).
