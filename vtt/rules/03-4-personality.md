# Personality — Complete Reference (Step 4)
Source: printed pg.100–104 (PDF pg.108–112).

## How it works
"Your personality is your general demeanor. It also mechanically reflects how you react when under pressure — represented by your status dice — and how your presence affects the other heroes even after you've been taken out."

At this step you also **create a custom quality** based on your hero's backstory — a quality that sums up your hero in a way not already covered by your existing qualities (this is the "Special" / Roleplaying Quality mentioned in Powers_and_Qualities.md — full explanation on pg.121, not yet transcribed).

Your Archetype provides the dice to roll for Personality selection (generally two d8s, per the standard progression, though this can vary by archetype). Use a single die or the sum of two.

**Each Personality entry provides:**
- A prompt to make up a quality based on your hero's backstory, with a die assigned to it
- Your **Status Dice** — the actual Green/Yellow/Red die sizes your hero uses for the GYRO track (this is the mechanical core of the step — personality directly determines your combat status dice)
- An **Out Ability** — what your hero can still do for the team even after being taken out of a scene
- Instruction to choose two Red Abilities (Step 5) from the next section

## Worked Example — Time-Slinger's Archetype completion + Personality (continuing the running example)

**Finishing Reality Shaper (Archetype 18):** Christopher has a d8 and d8+d8 to assign. One d8 goes to **Awareness**, the other to **Postcognition** — he stops gaining powers there and uses the remaining d8 for a quality, taking **Self-Discipline** at d8.

Selecting Green abilities: he takes **Probability Insight**, renamed **"Takin' My Time"**, tied to Power Arm:
> Boost using Power Arm. Use your Max die. If you roll doubles, you may also Attack using your Mid die.

And **Not Quite Right** becomes **"Altered Scan"** (no power required, since the ability doesn't specify one needed):
> After a die pool is rolled, adjust one die up or down one value on the die.

For the Yellow ability, he takes **Never Happened**, renamed **"Stack the Deck"**:
> When a nearby enemy would create a bonus or penalty, you may remove it immediately.

For his principle, Christopher looks at the Expertise principles list and takes **"Principle of Whispers,"** representing the robot AI that runs Jim's cybernetics.

**Personality:** Rolling d8+d8 from the archetype step produces **1 and 9**, granting the choice of **Lone Wolf (1)**, **Inquisitive (9)**, or **Alluring (10)** (via the sum). Christopher picks **Lone Wolf** — "Jim isn't the type to react to pressure," and it still fits "even when he's no longer hopping around in time."

Christopher records d8 for Jim's Green, Yellow, and Red status dice (Lone Wolf uses d8/d8/d8 uniformly — the simplest, most balanced spread on the chart).

For his custom quality, Christopher wants Jim's former life as Sheriff to matter, plus his "fish out of time" nature. He lands on **"Time-Lost Sheriff"** at d8, recorded under Qualities.

His Out Ability ties to **History**: **"Boost an ally by rolling your single History die."**

Then he moves on to Step 5, Red Abilities.
