# Archetypes — Complete Reference, All 20 Entries (Step 3)
Source: printed pg.72–98. Green/Yellow table order confirmed by directly reading a page image (Speedster) — for this chapter, the first table encountered is always Green, the second is always Yellow (opposite order from Power Sources). Merged from the former `03-3-archetypes-part1.md` (entries 1–16) and `03-3-archetypes-part2.md` (entries 17–20) into one file covering all 20.

## How it works
At the end of the Power Source step, use a single die's value or the sum of two to pick an Archetype 1–20. Each grants: a required Power/Quality assignment (often "skip or swap if you already have it"), more powers/qualities to assign remaining dice to, Green abilities (usually pick some from a list), sometimes a bonus Yellow ability, a Principle category, and new dice for Step 4 (Personality).

## Full Entries

### 1 — Speedster
*"Sorry gotta go be in twelve places at once..."*
Assign 1 die to Speed (skip/swap if already owned). Remaining dice → Agility, Intangibility, Lightning Calculator, Vitality, any Mobility power / any Mental or Physical quality.
**Green (pick 2, different power/quality):** Always on the Move (A) — Attack using [power/quality], Defend yourself Min die. Fast Fingers (A) — Boost/Hinder Max die, doubles = also Attack Mid die. Non-stop Assault (A) — Attack multiple targets Min die, Hinder each Mid die.

### 14 — Minion-Maker
*"Who needs friends when you can just make them?"*
Dice → Duplication, Inventions, Part Detachment, Robotics, any Elemental/Energy/Materials power / any Information or Mental quality.
**Green (both granted, each a different power):** Make Minion (A) — create a minion sized off the effect die (see chart below), choose its one basic action; acts at start of your turn, only in a fitting situation. Power Up (A) — Boost another hero or your minion, Max die or persistent Mid die.
**Yellow (pick 1):** Minion Formation (R) — reduce damage by minion count, shrink one minion per use. Rapid Deployment (A) — create a Min-die minion, acts now and every future turn-start. Upgrade Minion (A) — Boost a minion, may upgrade it to your Max die size.

### 19 — Divided
*"You have two very different forms, such as an unpowered civilian form and a powered heroic form."*
**Note from the book:** Divided heroes are more complex to create than most heroes. Strongly recommended that the *first* hero you create in SCRPG isn't a Divided hero.

Divided heroes have two forms: one civilian, one heroic, with some method to change between them. **Reroll the dice** you rolled at the end of the Power Source step and choose **another archetype** other than Divided or Modular. Your full archetype becomes **"Divided [whatever you chose]."** Follow all steps of that archetype, but when told to select a principle from it, return to this entry to complete this section instead. Consider notes on your auxiliary sheet for how your divided form works.

**First, choose ONE method of transformation:**

- **Controllable Transition** — you control the change at all times (magic word, transformation sequence). *Disadvantage: always takes time.* Gain: **Transform** (A) — Change from civilian to heroic form, or vice versa.
- **Device Transition** — you transform via a device (cybernetic upgrade, magical artifact). *Disadvantage: need access to the device.* Gain: **Device Transform** (A) — If you have access to your device, change forms. After transformation, take a basic action using Min die. If penalties separate you from your device, you cannot use this ability.
- **Merging/Possession Transition** — your transformation requires another entity. Gain ONE of:
  - **Merge** (A) — With a willing compatible person, change forms. After, take a basic action using Min die. Penalties separating you from that person block this ability.
  - **Possess Person** (A) — Attack using [a power gained from your archetype]. If you incapacitate the target and change forms, use Min die as a bonus to your next action. Alternatively possess a willing target for no bonus.
  - **Possess Object** (A) — Overcome using [a quality gained from your archetype]. On success, merge with an item, then use Min die to take a basic action.
- **Uncontrollable Transition** — you transform in response to stress, whether you want to or not. Gain: **Uncontrolled Transform** (A/I) — The first time you take damage or change zones in a scene, you must change from civilian to heroic form. You also transform by taking an action and taking damage equal to a roll of your current status die. After the action scene, you change back to civilian form.

**After choosing your transformation method, gain ONE of these Green abilities:**
- **Divided Psyche** (I) — While in civilian form, use two qualities instead of a power and a quality. While in heroic form, use two powers instead. (Use status in both cases.) You cannot use abilities related to a power/quality you don't have access to.
- **Split Form** (I) — Choose two powers and two qualities you always have access to in either form. Divide the remainder between civilian and heroic forms — usable only within those forms. Your Step 4 roleplaying quality is accessible in either form.

**Last:** instead of the principle from your other archetype, take a **Responsibility** principle.
Roll for personality selection. Optionally, take **two different personalities** — one per form — and adjust status dice accordingly (you still only have one roleplaying quality and one Out ability).

### 20 — Modular
*"You have multiple forms (configurations, fighting styles, etc.) that each provide their own advantages and disadvantages."*
**Note from the book:** Modular heroes are much more complex to create than most heroes. Strongly recommended that the *first* hero you create in SCRPG isn't a Modular hero.

Modular heroes have multiple **modes** defined during this process. First, **choose another archetype** — either another option from the dice you rolled for Archetype, or reroll and choose your base archetype, which becomes "modularized." Assign powers/qualities as listed in that archetype, but **skip gaining abilities from it** — come back here instead. If you have fewer than four powers, add one or two d6 powers of your choice until you have four.

**Fixed abilities, gained automatically:**
- Green — **Switch** (A) — Boost yourself using [power/quality]. Then change modes.
- Yellow — **Quick Switch** (A) — Destroy one bonus on you. Change modes, then take an action in the new mode.
- Red — **Emergency Switch** (R) — When hit with an Attack, you may change to any mode. If you do, take extra damage equal to the Min die, or take a minor twist.

**You have two Green modes** including one default mode, plus optionally a free powerless mode if it fits your concept. Record all modes on your auxiliary sheet.
- **Default Mode** — assign dice per your chosen archetype. No additional abilities in this mode.
- **Powerless Mode** (optional) — choose one power from default mode at d6, another at d8. Take this if there are circumstances separating you from your power source (e.g. a Power Suit). While in this mode, you cannot use any abilities except those from your Principles.

**Choose ONE more Green mode:**
- **Debilitator Mode** — pick 4 powers from default mode: 1 same size, 1 decreased (min d4), 2 increased (max d12). Cannot Boost/Defend/Overcome. Gain: **Debilitator** (A) — Hinder all nearby opponents using [power]. If doubles, take damage equal to Max die, then may also Attack all nearby opponents with Min die.
- **Improvement Mode** — pick 4 powers: 2 same size, 2 increased one size (max d12). Cannot Attack/Hinder. Gain: **Improvement** (A) — Boost yourself using [power]. Create one bonus at Max die and one at Mid die, both persistent and exclusive.
- **Scout Mode** — pick 4 powers: 2 same size, 1 decreased (min d4), 1 increased (max d12). Cannot Attack/Boost. Gain: **Scout** (A) — Overcome using [power]. Defend yourself with Max die. Then may end up anywhere in the current scene.

**Choose TWO Yellow modes from:**
- **Analysis Mode** — pick 4 powers: 2 same size, 2 increased one size (max d12). Cannot Attack/Defend. Gain: **Analysis** (A) — Hinder, or use one of your principles to Overcome, using [power]. Use Max+Min dice.
- **Bombardment Mode** — pick 3 powers: 2 decreased (min d4), 1 set to d12. Cannot Boost/Hinder/Overcome. Gain: **Bombardment** (A) — Defend yourself using [power]. May Attack one target with Max die.
- **Regeneration Mode** — pick 2 powers: 1 same size, 1 increased two sizes (max d12). Cannot Attack/Hinder. Gain: **Regeneration** (A) — Defend using [power]. Use Max die. Recover Health equal to Min die.
- **Skirmish Mode** — pick 4 powers: 1 same size, 1 decreased (min d4), 2 increased (max d12). Cannot Boost/Defend/Overcome. Gain: **Skirmish** (A) — Attack one target using [power]. Attack a different target with Min die. May change modes at end of turn.
- **Stalwart Mode** — pick 4 powers: 1 same size, 2 decreased (min d4), 1 increased two sizes (max d12). Cannot Hinder/Overcome. Gain: **Stalwart** (A) — Defend yourself and all nearby allies using [power] against each Attack until the beginning of your next turn.

**Choose ONE Red mode from:**
- **Destroyer Mode** — pick 3 powers: 2 same size, 1 increased one size (max d12). Immobile, cannot Boost. Gain: **Destroyer** (I) — Whenever you take a basic Attack action, either use Max+Min dice on one target, or Attack two different targets (Max die one, Mid die other).
- **Hunter/Killer Mode** — pick 2 powers: both increased one size (max d12). Cannot Defend/Overcome. Gain: **Hunter/Killer** (A) — Move to any target in the scene, Hinder using [power], then Attack that target using Max+Min dice.
- **Shield Mode** — pick 4 powers: 2 same size, 2 increased one size (max d12). Cannot Attack. Gain: **Shield** (R) — When attacked, Defend by rolling your single [power] die. If damage reduced to 0 or less, may also Hinder the source using the result of the die.

Once modes are assigned, choose a principle from your other (modularized) archetype. Roll for personality selection.
