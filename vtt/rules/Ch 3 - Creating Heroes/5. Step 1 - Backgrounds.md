## Step 1 - Backgrounds

Your background determines where your hero came from before they became a hero. 

To begin, roll d10 d10. Using the value of one of the two d10s you just rolled, or by adding them together, select a background, recording it in the Characteristics section of your hero sheet. That background provides you with some of your qualities (explained on page 15), a principle (explained on page 14), and a new set of dice to roll for the power source section. 

For help with how to gain qualities and assign dice, check out pages 44-45.


1. **Upper Class** — *You were born in the upper echelons of high society. Likely you’re also pretty darn rich.*
	Assign d10 and d8 to two of these qualities:
	- Fitness
	- Persuasion
	- Select from Mental qualities category
	Choose a Responsibility principle (pages 138-141).
	Roll d10 and d8 d8 for power source selection.
2. **Blank Slate** — *You remember nothing. Were you brainwashed? Maybe you were just created? One way or another, you have no history. You start now. Your future is what you make of it.*
	Assign d10 and d8 to two of these qualities:
	- Select from Mental qualities category
	- Select from Physical qualities category
	Choose an Identity principle (pages 135-137).
	Roll d10 and d8 d8 for power source selection.
