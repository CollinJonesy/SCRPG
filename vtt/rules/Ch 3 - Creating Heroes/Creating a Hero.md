# Creating a Hero


Character creation in SCRPG is a **fixed-order assembly**; the book presents it as **8 numbered steps**
(Backgrounds, Power Sources, Archetypes, Personalities, Red Abilities, **Retcon**, **Health**, **Finishing
Touches**), with Principles established during Steps 1 and 3 as a separate unnumbered thread. The order
below is the book's actual sequence (Backgrounds p49 → Power Sources p57 → Archetypes p73 → Personalities
p101 → Red Abilities p106 → …), confirmed against the rulebook text.


## The steps (actual book order)
1. **Background** (p49) — pick your upbringing/role. Grants Qualities + a Principle Category, and dice
   (the "Dice" column) you roll for the next step. *See [[07_Backgrounds_and_Personalities]].*
2. **Power Source** (p57) — pick one of 20 sources. Grants Powers + innate qualities, and the dice you'll
   assign. *See [[06_Power_Sources]].*
3. **Archetype** (p73) — pick one of 20. Grants a Default mode (Green/Yellow/Red abilities) + a Principle
   type. *See [[08_Archetypes]].*
4. **Personality** (p101) — pick one. Sets your **GYRO status dice** (Green/Yellow/Red). *See
   [[07_Backgrounds_and_Personalities]].*
5. **Red Abilities** (p106) — choose your Red-zone abilities from a shared list (your desperation moves).
6. **Retcon** — a mid-game "do-over" mechanic (step 6 of 8).
7. **Health** — finalize your Health numbers (step 7 of 8).
8. **Finishing Touches** — choose a hero name, etc. (step 8 of 8).
   Principles are woven in during Steps 1 and 3 (your Background and Archetype each set a Principle type).


> **Why order matters:** the dice you roll at the end of one step are *assigned* in the next (e.g. the dice
> rolled for Power Source selection are assigned to a power during the Archetype step). Running the steps out
> of order produces a broken hero sheet.


## Powers vs. Qualities
- **Powers** = the things you can do (Flight, Energy Blasts, Telepathy) — explicitly *including* non-
  supernatural origins such as genetics or "just old fashioned training," not only superpowers. Granted
  mainly by your Power Source, expanded by Background/Archetype.
- **Qualities** = *who you are* (Athletic, Intellectual, any Social/Mental/Physical/Information). Broad;
  usable for many actions. Backgrounds and Principles add these. Each Quality has a die (🎲d6–🎲d12).
- Both are rolled as part of a dice pool (one Power + one Quality + status die).


## Status (GYRO) for heroes
Set by your **Personality** (see the Personalities Quick Reference). Example: **Stalwart = 🎲d8/🎲d8/🎲d8**
(Green/Yellow/Red). Your status die is what you add to every pool.


## Modular and Default modes
Your Archetype grants a **Default mode** (always-on suite of abilities). **Modular mode** is a feature of
*specifically* the "Modular" archetype (one of the 20) — mode-switching happens via an in-combat ability
during play, not by swapping loadouts "between Issues." Do not assume every archetype offers Modular modes.


## Principles
- **Ideals** — what the hero believes (bonus when you act on them).
- **Responsibilities** — what the hero is obligated to (bonus when upheld, penalty when neglected).
- The *type* of Principle (Identity / Expertise / Responsibility / Ideals / Esoteric) is set by your
  Background, which dictates which list you draw from.
