# Archetypes


An **Archetype** (Step 3 of hero creation, after Power Source) is the hero's *role/identity template* — it
grants a **Default mode** (a suite of Green/Yellow/Red abilities you always have) and a **Principle type**.
The list below is the book's **actual 20 Archetypes** (Archetypes Chart, p73–98), recovered from the
rulebook text.

## Why archetypes matter
Powers and Backgrounds say *what you can do*; the Archetype says *how you fight and what your signature
moves are*. Two heroes with the same Power Source can feel totally different via Archetype.


## Default vs. Modular modes
- **Default mode:** always available. Usually 1 Green, 1–2 Yellow, 1–2 Red abilities.
- **Modular mode (e.g. for the Modular archetype):** pick a loadout, reassign dice among your powers for the
  scene, then regain it next Issue. Lets one hero flex between "tank," "blaster," or "support" across sessions.


## Status gating
Abilities are restricted to a zone: a **Green** ability only works while you're Green; a **Red** ability is
your desperation move. See [[03_GYRO_Status]].
