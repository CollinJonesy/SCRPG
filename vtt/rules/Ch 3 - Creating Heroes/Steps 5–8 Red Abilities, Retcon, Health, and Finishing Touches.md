 
The final stretch of [[04_Hero_Creation_Overview|Hero Creation]], following [[08_Personalities|Step 4: Personalities]]. These four steps round out a hero: pick their signature Red abilities, allow one late tweak to the build, calculate Health, and fill in narrative/cosmetic details.


---


## Step 5: Red Abilities


At the end of [[08_Personalities|Step 4]] every personality instructs you to **choose two Red abilities** from a single shared list. That list is organized into categories that mirror the power and quality categories from [[07_Archetypes|Archetypes]] / [[10_Powers_Qualities_and_Principles]]:


**Power categories:** Athletic, Elemental/Energy, Hallmark, Intellectual, Materials, Mobility, Psychic, Self Control, Technological.


**Quality categories:** Information, Mental, Physical, Social.


Rules for choosing:


- The Red abilities in each category are grouped there for a mechanical reason, not just convenience: **to take an ability from a category, you must have a die assigned to a power or quality in that category.**
- If an ability doesn't reference a power or quality at all (this applies to most **Inherent**-type abilities), you instead need a power or quality rated **d8 or higher** in that category to qualify for it.
- Some Red abilities are further restricted to a *specific* power or quality (e.g., "Signature Vehicle" or "Signature Weaponry") rather than any power in the category.


Each ability entry in the source lists an icon/type marker — **A** (Ability action), **R** (Reaction), or **I** (Inherent/passive) — a name, and game text describing its effect (e.g., *Powerful Strike*: Attack using [power], use your Max+Mid dice; *Impenetrable Defense*: Defend using [power] with Max+Mid dice against all Attacks until your next turn; *Major Regeneration*: Hinder yourself using [power]/Min die, Recover Health equal to Max+Mid dice). Once chosen, an ability is typically renamed and reflavored to match the hero — in the worked example, the Psychic-category ability *Give Time* was renamed *Temporal Bootstrap* and reskinned to use the character's Postcognition power, and the Mental-category ability *Final Wrath* became *Showdown*, reskinned to Self-Discipline.

## Step 6: Retcon


"Retcon" (retroactive continuity) is a single, late tweak to the hero — a chance to fix or refine something before finalizing. Choose **one** of the following:


- Swap any two dice within your powers
- Swap any two dice within your qualities
- Choose a different power or quality used in one of your abilities
- Add any d6 power or quality from any category
- Increase your Red status die by one size (maximum d12)
- Change either of your principles to any other principle
- Gain an extra Red ability, as described in Step 5


In the worked example, the retcon is used to gain an extra Red ability — taking *Quick Exit* and renaming/reskinning it as *Get Out of Dodge*, applied to the hero's Robot Horse power.

## Step 7: Health


Total Health is the sum of four values:


1. **8** (flat base)
2. **The maximum value of your Red status die**
3. **The maximum value of any one of your Athletic powers or Mental qualities** (use **d4** if you have none of either)
4. **The roll of a d8 or a flat 4** — you choose which before rolling


> ⚠️ Note: the Red status die value used in the worked example calculation is itself one of the values affected by the OCR/chart-conflict issue documented in [[08_Personalities]] (Lone Wolf is listed as d8 in the chart). The worked example computes 8 + 8 (Red status die) + 10 (Mental quality: Self‑Discipline) + 4 (rolled d8, got a 4) = **30** — but the Red status die's "8" here should be treated with the same caution as the rest of that table until confirmed against the physical book.


Once you have your total Health, look it up on the Health chart to find your **Green**, **Yellow**, and **Red** Health ranges (the chart runs from a total of 17 up through 40, each row splitting the total into three descending range bands). For example, a total of 30 splits into Green 30–23, Yellow 22–12, Red 11–1; a total of 40 splits into Green 40–30, Yellow 29–15, Red 14–1. Record your Green range at the top, then Yellow, then Red — these are the health thresholds your hero moves through in play (Green = healthy, Yellow = wounded, Red = critical) before being taken out.

## Step 8: Finishing Touches


The last step is narrative and cosmetic, filling in the top of the hero sheet:


- **Hero Name** and **Alias** (heroic identity vs. civilian name)
- **Physical Attributes**: Gender, Age, and Height, plus notes on Eyes, Hair, Skin, and Build
- **Costume/Equipment**: a brief description of what the hero wears/carries in the field
- Optional sketch or reference image


The book is explicit that these fields are flexible shorthand, not rigid categories — a bald hero can write "none" under Hair, an energy-based hero might write "bioluminescent" under Skin, an alien hero might have "seven" Eyes, and Age can be as loose as "mid-thirties." Gender is not limited to "F"/"M" — use whatever accurately describes the hero. None of these details are permanent; heroes can and do change over time, and the fields exist to give a useful shorthand for talking about the character, not to lock them in.


Finally, if any chosen abilities haven't been renamed yet, this is the point to rename them to fit the hero's theme, filling in the Name slots on the hero sheet.


Completing this step means the hero is finished. In the worked example, "Chrono-Ranger" becomes **Time-Slinger** (alias Jim Brooks): M, Middle-Aged, 5'11", Brown eyes, Brown hair, Tan skin, Rugged build, wearing a cowboy hat, worn jeans, brown leather boots, a blue collared shirt with golden clock-arm emblem, a golden left arm, a golden time-gun, a glowing blue eyepiece, and riding a robot horse named Masadah.
