# Backgrounds and Personalities


Two creation steps define **who the hero is outside their powers**: the **Background** (Step 1 — upbringing/
role, grants Qualities + a Principle Category + a die for the next step) and the **Personality** (Step 4 —
temperament, which sets your GYRO status dice). Both are recovered from the rulebook's actual charts
(Backgrounds p49–54, Personalities p100–101).


## Backgrounds (the 20, with granted Qualities + Principle Category)
The **Chart Dice** column is the single die the Backgrounds Chart lists for each background. Only **5 of 20**
backgrounds have a die listed in the chart (Anachronistic, Exile, Former Villain, Interstellar, Dynasty);
the other 15 list none.


> **Important — the selection roll is multi-die, not the chart column.** The Background step has you
> **roll multiple dice** to select a Power Source. This roll comes from the step text / worked examples,
> NOT the chart's single "Dice" column. Confirmed physical-book examples: the Time-Slinger character
> (Anachronistic) rolls **d10/d8/d6**; **Retired** rolls **d12/d6/d6** (confirmed from the physical book:
> Assign 🎲d10 & 🎲d10, Roll 🎲d12/🎲d6/🎲d6). The chart's single "Dice" value appears to be a summary/
> signature die, not the selection roll.

## Out abilities
Each Personality also grants an **Out ability** (what happens when you're taken Out) and you choose two Red
abilities (Step 5) from a shared list.
