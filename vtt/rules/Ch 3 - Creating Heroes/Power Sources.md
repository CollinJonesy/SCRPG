# Step 2: Power Sources
1. **Accident** — An external source caused your powers to manifest, possibly from a cure gone wrong. Assign your dice to any of: Athletic, Elemental/Energy, Intellectual, Materials, Psychic, or Self Control categories. Pick two Yellow abilities (different powers) and one Green ability.
2. **Training** — Your powers are the result of hard work, dedication, and long hours. Assign dice to Gadgets, Signature Vehicle, Signature Weaponry, or the Athletic/Intellectual categories. Pick two Yellow abilities (different powers). Instead of a standard Green ability, you select an extra quality from your archetype's list when you reach that step.
3. **Genetic** — Mutations in your DNA caused unusual abilities to develop. Assign dice to Agility, Flight, Signature Weaponry, Strength, Vitality, or the Intellectual/Psychic categories. Pick two Yellow abilities (different powers) and one Green ability.
4. **Experimentation** — Your powers were created in a lab, with unexpected side effects. Assign dice to Signature Weaponry, or the Athletic, Elemental/Energy, Intellectual, Mobility, or Self Control categories. Pick two Yellow abilities (different powers) and one Green ability.
5. **Mystical** — Magical training or an alteration by magic gave you your powers. Assign dice to Awareness, Flight, Presence, Signature Weaponry, Teleportation, or the Elemental/Energy, Materials, Psychic, or Self Control categories. Pick two Yellow abilities (different powers). Instead of a Green ability, you gain an Information quality and assign a die to it.
6. **Nature** — The power of nature flows through you. Assign dice to Animal Control, Cold, Electricity, Fire, Flight, Leaping, Shapeshifting, Swimming, Swinging, Wall-Crawling, Weather, or the Athletic/Materials categories. Pick two Yellow abilities and one Green ability.
7. **Relic** — An object (or collection of objects) of mystical significance grants or altered you to have powers. Assign dice to Awareness, Intuition, Signature Vehicle, Signature Weaponry, or the Elemental/Energy, Materials, Mobility, Psychic, or Self Control categories. Pick two Yellow abilities (different powers) and one Green ability.
8. **Powered Suit** — An engineered suit provides your powers and may be vital to keeping you alive. Assign one die specifically to the Power Suit power (required), then assign your remaining dice to Awareness, Cold, Elasticity, Electricity, Fire, Lightning Calculator, Nuclear, Part Detachment, Signature Vehicle, Signature Weaponry, or the Athletic/Mobility categories. Pick two Yellow abilities (different powers) and one Green ability.
9. **Radiation** — Exposure to radiation charged your system and granted new abilities. Assign dice to Nuclear, Signature Vehicle, Signature Weaponry, or the Athletic, Self Control, or Technological categories. Pick two Yellow abilities and one Green ability.
10. **Tech Upgrades** — Technological upgrades and implants give you your powers. Assign dice to Signature Vehicle, Signature Weaponry, or the Athletic, Elemental/Energy, Intellectual, Mobility, or Technological categories. Pick two Yellow abilities (different powers) and one Green ability.
11. **Supernatural** — In some way you pierced the veil of life and reality and brought back power. Assign dice to Awareness, Cold, Electricity, Fire, Infernal, Plants, Presence, Radiant, Strength, Transmutation, Vitality, Weather, or the Mobility, Psychic, or Self Control categories. Pick two Yellow abilities. Instead of a standard Green ability, you gain one power not on the above list and assign it a d10.
12. **Artificial Being** — You were created, not born, and your abilities stem from your makeup. Assign dice to Inventions, Robotics, Signature Vehicle, Signature Weaponry, or the Athletic, Elemental/Energy, Intellectual, Mobility, or Self Control categories. Pick two Yellow abilities (different powers) and one Green ability.
13. **Cursed** — A supernatural curse was inflicted on you or your family line, granting both boons and banes. Assign dice to Signature Weaponry, or the Athletic, Elemental/Energy, Materials, or Self Control categories. Pick two Yellow abilities and one Green ability.
14. **Alien** — You're not from Earth, though your powers may not be unusual where you're from, or you were granted abilities by an extraterrestrial source. Assign dice to Signature Vehicle, Signature Weaponry, or the Athletic, Elemental/Energy, Intellectual, Mobility, Psychic, or Technological categories. Pick two Yellow abilities. Instead of a Green ability, you upgrade one existing power or quality by one die size — or, if you have no eligible power, add a new power from the list instead.
15. **Genius** — Your powers come from your brilliant mind; you've turned your staggering intellect to fighting crime. Assign dice to Inventions, Robotics, Signature Vehicle, Signature Weaponry, or the Intellectual category. Pick two Yellow abilities (different powers). Instead of a Green ability, you select an extra quality from the Information or Mental quality categories.
16. **Cosmos** — Exposure to forces from beyond the stars changed you. Assign dice to Cosmic, Intuition, Signature Vehicle, Signature Weaponry, or the Mobility, Psychic, Self Control, or Technological categories. Pick two Yellow abilities (different powers). Instead of a standard Green ability, you downgrade one power or quality by one die size and upgrade a different one by one die size.
17. **Extradimensional** — Exposure to side dimensions (such as the Realm of Discord) has left its mark on you. Assign dice to Cosmic, Duplication, Infernal, Intangibility, Invisibility, Radiant, Signature Vehicle, Signature Weaponry, Transmutation, Teleportation, or the Intellectual/Psychic categories. Pick two Yellow abilities and one Green ability.
18. **Unknown** — You don't know the source of your powers: they either manifested one day, or hint at a bigger mystery. Assign dice to the Elemental/Energy, Intellectual, Materials, Self Control, or Technological categories. Pick two Yellow abilities. Instead of a Green ability, you gain a Social quality at d4.
19. **Higher Power** — You've been chosen by a higher force or are a being from another realm; your powers reflect your calling or true form. Assign dice to the Athletic, Elemental/Energy, Materials, Psychic, or Self Control categories. Pick two Yellow abilities (different powers) and one Green ability.
20. **The Multiverse** — You've traveled or been flung through the realms of time and space. Assign dice to Awareness, Cosmic, Intuition, Speed, Teleportation, or the Psychic/Self Control categories. Pick two Yellow abilities. Instead of a standard Green ability, you gain one extra power from any category.
