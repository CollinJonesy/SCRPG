# Backgrounds


## What a Background Is


A Background is the first step of hero creation. It represents where your hero came from before they picked up the mask — regular life, military service, another world, another time, whatever shaped them before "hero" was part of the story.


Picking a Background does three things at once:
- Grants a small set of starting **Qualities** (you assign dice to specific named qualities or to "any [category]" slots the entry lists).
- Sets your **Principle Category** — the theme you'll pick your first Principle from.
- Hands you a **set of dice to roll** going into Step 2 (Power Source selection) — you roll those dice and use one die's value, or two added together, to pick your Power Source on that chart.


To choose a Background, roll the two dice specified by the Step 1 instructions (a d6 and d8 in the guided method) and use one die's result, or the sum of both, as the row number on the Backgrounds Chart below.


A handful of Backgrounds (marked in the Dice column below) additionally carry their own die icon printed right next to the background's name on the chart — those are a *bonus* die folded into the qualities/power-source dice for that specific background, on top of the normal per-entry dice described in its write-up.

### ⚠️ Retired — text anomaly


The Retired entry's extracted source text reads unusually different from every other Background write-up. Where every other entry just says "Assign [die icons] to two of these qualities... Roll [die icons] ... for power source selection" (with the actual die sizes only visible as icons, not spelled out in words), the Retired entry's extracted text explicitly spells out sizes:


> "Assign 🎲d10 and 🎲d10 / Roll 🎲d12, 🎲d6, and 🎲d6 / to two of these qualities: • Select from Information qualities category • Select from Social qualities category / Choose an Identity principle (pages 135-137). / Roll [icons] and [icons] for power source selection."


This is quoted exactly as extracted. No other of the 20 backgrounds spells out die sizes in prose this way, which suggests a text-extraction artifact (icon alt-text leaking into the paragraph) rather than a genuine rules difference. Treat the specific die values "d10/d10" and "d12/d6/d6" for Retired as **⚠️ Needs physical-book confirmation** rather than trusting them at face value.


## Background Flavor


A short paraphrase of each Background's flavor blurb from the source text:


1. **Upper Class** — Born into high society, likely with money to match.
2. **Blank Slate** — No memory of a past; whatever came before is gone, and the hero's story starts now.
3. **Struggling** — Life has been an uphill climb, low on resources and luck, doing the best with what little there was.
4. **Adventurer** — A lifelong thrill-seeker who chased excitement long before becoming a hero.
5. **Unremarkable** — An ordinary life, until something extraordinary changed everything.
6. **Law Enforcement** — Cop, detective, lawyer, or judge — someone who built a career around the law.
7. **Academic** — A teacher, professor, researcher, or clergy member devoted to the pursuit of knowledge.
8. **Tragic** — Defined by a major loss or tragedy that both haunts and drives them.
9. **Performer** — Born for the stage, with a strong sense of how they present themselves to the world.
10. **Military** — Combat-trained, likely through organized armed forces, possibly with real combat experience.
11. **Retired** — Once a hero who hung up the cape, now pulled back into the fight by changing circumstances.
12. **Criminal** — Once on the wrong side of the law, now turning that experience toward heroism.
13. **Medical** — A doctor, nurse, or veterinarian with deep experience treating injury and disease.
14. **Anachronistic** — Out of step with their own time — a stranded time traveler or someone who fits an earlier or later era.
15. **Exile** — Far from home, whether by choice or by being sent away, now making a life elsewhere.
16. **Former Villain** — Once an enemy of heroes, now reformed — though trust from other heroes is still hard-won.
17. **Interstellar** — A newcomer to Earth from beyond the stars, alien or otherwise, still learning the planet's customs.
18. **Dynasty** — Part of a line of heroes, whether by blood or by adoption into a hero family.
19. **Otherworldly** — Touched by the uncanny or supernatural, whether fully so or as the offspring of something not entirely human.
20. **Created** — A constructed being, built by someone or something, still drawn to the role of hero despite different origins than an organic creature.


## Worked Example: Anachronistic (Time-Slinger)


The source rulebook walks through Jim Brooks (formerly the hero Chrono-Ranger) being rebuilt as a "man out of time." His player rolls two dice for the background selection and gets 6 and 8; individually those point to Law Enforcement (6) and Tragic (8), but adding them together (14) lands on **Anachronistic**, which fits the character's story better, so that's what gets chosen.


From there, two qualities are assigned: History and Ranged Combat. The write-up later shows these recorded as **History d10** and **Ranged Combat d8**.


⚠️ **Flagged inconsistency:** when the example proceeds into Power Source selection, the dice from Anachronistic produce options including "Genetic 🎲d8 (3)" in the worked-example prose — but the Power Sources Chart itself, quoted directly from the source, lists the Genetic entry's die as **🎲d10** ("GENETIC 🎲d10"). These two values conflict directly in the source text as extracted. This is called out here rather than resolved — treat the Genetic Power Source's chart die as ⚠️ Needs physical-book confirmation until checked against a physical copy.
