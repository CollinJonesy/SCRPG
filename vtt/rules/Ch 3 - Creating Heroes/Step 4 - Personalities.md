## What Personality does


Personality is Step 4 of [[04_Hero_Creation_Overview|Hero Creation]], following [[07_Archetypes|Archetypes]]. It covers three things at once:


1. **Sets your Green/Yellow/Red status dice** — a personality entry comes with a trio of status dice that represent how your hero reacts to escalating pressure over the course of a fight (the GYR track used elsewhere in the system).
2. **Grants a unique quality.** You invent a quality based on your hero's backstory — something not already captured by your existing qualities — and assign a die to it (the "Special" quality, described on p.121 of the source book).
3. **Grants an Out ability.** Every personality has a unique action your hero can perform even after being taken out of the fight (reduced to the Red zone / knocked out), usually a Boost, Hinder, or Defend rolled with a single die.


### Picking a personality


Your [[07_Archetypes|Archetype]] specifies which dice to roll (typically two dice, or one die used twice) against the 20-entry Personalities table to generate two numbers 1–20. You then choose freely between the two personalities those numbers indicate. In the worked example, a roll produces 1 and 9, offering a choice between Lone Wolf (1) and Inquisitive (9) — plus, per the surrounding text, Alluring (10) is also mentioned as an option, suggesting the roll may generate a small range/nearby options rather than a single strict pair. This mechanic is likely clarified further elsewhere in the chapter; treat the exact selection rule as provisional until cross-checked.


Every personality entry also comes with the same closing instruction: **choose two Red abilities** from the shared list described in [[09_Red_Abilities_and_Finishing|Step 5: Red Abilities]].

- Because literally every checkable personality (2 through 20, save Stalwart/Sarcastic/Lone Wolf where one side is simply missing) shows a chart-vs-write-up conflict, this is not isolated to Natural Leader and Decisive — it appears to be a systemic OCR failure across the whole table. Do not treat any single value in this table as reliable without checking the physical book or a clean scan.


## Out Abilities


Each personality's Out ability is a single-die action (Boost, Hinder, or Defend, usually rolling either the new custom [quality] die, a [power] die, or the Red status die) that your hero can still perform after being taken out of the action. The specific ability text recovered from each write-up:


| Personality | Out Ability (as recovered) |
|---|---|
| Lone Wolf | Boost an ally by rolling your single [quality] die. |
| Natural Leader | Boost an ally by rolling your single [quality] die. |
| Impulsive | The hero who goes directly after you may take 1 damage to reroll their dice pool. |
| Mischievous | Hinder an opponent by rolling your single [power] die. |
| Sarcastic | Hinder an opponent by rolling your single [quality] die. |
| Distant | Boost an ally by rolling your single Red status die. |
| Stalwart | Defend an ally by rolling your single [power] die. |

| Inquisitive | Choose an ally. Until your next turn, that ally may reroll one of their dice by using a Reaction. |
| Alluring | Boost an ally by rolling your single [power] die. |
| Stoic | Defend an ally by rolling your single [quality] die. |
| Nurturing | Boost an ally by rolling your single [quality] die. |
| Analytical | Remove a bonus or penalty of your choice. |
| Decisive | Boost an ally by rolling your single [power] die. |

These ability texts read cleanly and are not in conflict anywhere in the source, so they are treated as reliable, unlike the status-die values above.
