Free For All

1 · Background (p49–54)

Constructed: pick a Background. Assign its quality dice (the listed sizes — not a free pick of any die). Choose a Principle from its category. Then take the listed dice to Power Source — you choose the source; there is no selection roll (that is Guided only).

2 · Power Source (p57–70)

Constructed: pick a Power Source (same card + assign layout as Background). Put the dice you brought from Background onto this source’s powers. Then pick its Green/Yellow abilities. This source also grants dice forward to Archetype (sidecar).

3 · Archetype (p73–98)

Constructed: pick an Archetype. Assign the dice you brought from Power Source onto its offered powers/qualities. Pick Green/Yellow abilities from its list. Choose its Principle when the book gives a category.

4 · Personality (p100–112)

Constructed: pick a Personality (GYRO status dice are locked from the book). Name the custom quality it grants and assign its listed die. The Out ability text is from this Personality — bind [quality] / [power] to one you already assigned.

5 · Red Abilities (p106 / pp.114–119)

Verbatim (p114): "Choose two Red abilities. The Red abilities are grouped by power and quality category… You must have a die assigned to a power or quality in a category to use with an ability from that category. If an ability doesn't use a power or quality at all (like most Inherent abilities), you must have a power or quality rated d6 or higher in that category in order to take it. Some Red abilities also specify that they can only be used with a specific power or quality."

Verbatim (p122): "All of these details go at the top of your hero's sheet: there's space for your Hero Name and Alias, as well as a Physical Attributes section. Finally, briefly detail the Costume/Equipment… If you haven't renamed your abilities yet, go over them and come up with some snappy names that fit your hero."
